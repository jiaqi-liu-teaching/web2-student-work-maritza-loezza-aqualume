INSIDE AQUALUME

Aqualume is a modern aquarium where visitors can explore
different marine environments and experience ocean life up close.

Each exhibit highlights a range of species and the spaces they live in, while the layout stays open and easy to move through so you can take your time.

EXPLORE THE AQUARIUM

As you move through Aqualume, you’ll come across moments that invite you to slow down, look closer, and take in what’s around you.

From watching jellyfish drift through glowing tanks to stepping into spaces surrounded by marine life, each area offers something a little different.

HOW IT’S MAINTAINED

Water Quality

Water is filtered and monitored to keep conditions clean, balanced, and safe for marine life.

Habitat Care

Each space is maintained to support stable, natural conditions for the animals inside.

Marine Well-Being

Marine life is supported through daily care, including feeding, health checks, and overall conditions.
